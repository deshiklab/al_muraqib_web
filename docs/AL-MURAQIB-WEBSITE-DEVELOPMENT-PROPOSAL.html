Executive Summary

This document outlines a streamlined development plan to modify, build, and launch a high-performance bilingual (English/Arabic) web platform for Al Muraqib Fiber Glass Industry LLC. Leveraging the prototype design already initiated by our team, our primary focus is on executing a robust frontend deployment and integrating a powerful backend Content Management System (CMS) with CRM-connected quotation workflows. This targeted approach ensures a scalable, cost-effective rollout that aligns with your operational priorities as a leading GRP/FRP manufacturer serving the UAE and GCC markets.

Project Understanding & Objectives

Based on our review of your current online presence, your product portfolio and the prototype design already under way, we understand your primary objectives are to:

  • Establish a Global B2B Presence: Deploy the prototype into a fully functional, credible bilingual website (English + Arabic with full RTL) showcasing your GRP panel tanks, septic tanks, plunge pools, linings, gratings and their industrial applications, together with your certifications and landmark project references.
  • Enhance Client Communication: Integrate modern communication tools — an RFQ/quotation wizard, WhatsApp click-to-chat and live chat — plus secure inquiry forms connected to your CRM, to facilitate seamless interactions with prospective international and local buyers.
  • Streamline Content Management: Implement a robust backend and headless CMS, allowing your team to easily update product catalogue pages, technical specifications & variants, certifications, projects and corporate news without technical bottlenecks.
  • Win Search Visibility: Deliver multilingual on-page SEO (hreflang, sitemaps, structured metadata) and fast, Core-Web-Vitals-friendly performance so buyers searching for GRP tanks and related products find you first.

Phased Development Plan & Deliverables

We will structure the project into focused phases to ensure a smooth technical transition and robust implementation of your provided design.

Phase 1: Frontend Adaptation & CMS Integration

Focus: To translate the initiated prototype into a fully responsive, bilingual frontend architecture and integrate a dynamic backend CMS that serves as the digital face of your manufacturing business.

KEY DELIVERABLES:

1. Frontend Development (Based on Provided Design):

  • Conversion of the prototype design into responsive, high-performance code (utilizing modern stacks such as Next.js (React) and Tailwind CSS).
  • Bilingual EN/AR with full RTL support, language switcher and Arabic typography.
  • Product catalogue with search & filters, product detail pages (gallery, tabs, technical specifications, variants, key features, applications, FAQ, related products), portfolio/projects showcase, certifications, blog and knowledge base.
  • RFQ (quotation) wizard, WhatsApp click-to-chat, tawk.to live chat, and light/dark theme toggle.
  • Optimization of UI elements to ensure cross-device compatibility (mobile, tablet, desktop) and fast loading times for global accessibility.

2. Backend & CMS Architecture:

  • Content Management: A secure admin panel (headless CMS) to create, edit, and delete content on all public-facing pages — products, specs/variants, projects, certifications, articles and pages.
  • Basic User Management: Ability to create and manage admin/editor roles.
  • Theme & UI Settings: Controls for basic site settings (contact details, banners, homepage sections).
  • Contact / RFQ Message Inbox & CRM Integration: A central place to receive and review all inquiries from the website's contact and quotation forms, with automatic push of quotation requests into your CRM.

Phase 2: Website Service & Maintenance

Focus: To integrate essential tools for team coordination, managing client relationships and streamlining the billing process.

KEY DELIVERABLES:

1. Regular Maintenance of the Website:

  • System Error & Bug Fixing.
