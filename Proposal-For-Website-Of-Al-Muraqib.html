Executive Summary

This document outlines a streamlined development plan to modify, build, and launch a high-performance web platform for Al Muraqib Fiber Glass Industry L.L.C. Leveraging the prototype already prepared by our team, our primary focus is on executing a robust frontend deployment and integrating a powerful backend Content Management System (CMS). This targeted approach ensures a scalable, cost-effective rollout that aligns with your operational priorities in GRP/FRP manufacturing, water storage, pools, lining systems and custom composite work.

Project Understanding & Objectives

Based on our review of the live site (almuraqib.ae), the prototype, and the company profile, we understand your primary objectives are to:

  • Establish a credible industrial B2B presence: Deploy the prototype into a fully functional website showcasing GRP/FRP products, factory capability, ISO / DM / ESMA credentials and landmark projects (FIVE Luxe JBR, FIVE Sensoria, Royal M Hotel Fujairah, and others).
  • Enhance client communication: Integrate quotation forms (with drawing / BOQ upload), WhatsApp and secure inquiry handling for contractors, consultants and procurement teams in the UAE and GCC.
  • Streamline content management: Implement a robust backend and CMS so your team can update product catalogues, project case studies, certifications and knowledge articles without technical bottlenecks.
  • Support bilingual reach: English for international tenders and Arabic (RTL) for the UAE market.

Phased Development Plan & Deliverables

We will structure the project into focused phases to ensure a smooth technical transition and robust implementation of the provided prototype design.

Phase 1: Frontend Adaptation & CMS Integration

Focus: To translate the approved prototype into a fully responsive frontend architecture and integrate a dynamic backend CMS that serves as the digital face of your manufacturing business.

Key deliverables:

1. Frontend Development (Based on Provided Design)

  • Conversion of the prototype into responsive, high-performance code (React and Tailwind CSS).
  • Optimization of UI elements for mobile, tablet and desktop, with fast loading for regional and international visitors.
  • Core pages: Home, About, Certifications, Products (six groups + detail), Services, Projects, Knowledge/Blog, Get a Quotation, Contact, Legal.
  • Product groups: Water Storage Tanks; Pools & Leisure; Lining & Protection; Septic & Sewerage; Architectural & Landscaping; Specialty Products.
  • Arabic / RTL layout as a first-class counterpart to English.

2. Backend & CMS Architecture

  • Content Management: A secure admin panel to create, edit and delete content on all public-facing pages (products, projects, articles, certificates, client logos).
  • Basic User Management: Ability to create and manage admin/editor roles.
  • Theme & UI Settings: Controls for basic site settings.
  • Contact / Quotation Inbox: A central place to receive and review inquiries and quotation requests, including file uploads.

Phase 2: Website Service & Maintenance

Focus: Ongoing reliability, content support and operational continuity after go-live.

Key deliverables:

1. Regular maintenance of the website

  • System error & bug fixing.
  • Help to manage users and admin panel.
  • Help to update and change the contents.

2. Maintaining the site

  • Site deploy.
  • Update after maintenance.
  • Restore from backup.
